## The Challenge by Tyks Laboratories

Title:
> Antibiotic efficacy indicator, AEI Development of a rolling, real-time, laboratory-based tool for e.g. antimicrobial resistance surveillance

Description:
> The WHO, the UN and the World Economic Forum have identified antimicrobial resistance (AMR) as one of the most serious global public health threats to humans. Tyks Laboratories carries out antimicrobial susceptibility testing and monitors bacterial resistance. Currently, these reports are compiled annually at hospital level, which is too imprecise and infrequent. When selecting antibiotic treatment for a patient, a doctor wants to see up-to-date information on the susceptibility profile of bacteria in their own ward. Develop a continuously updated tool for monitoring e.g. antimicrobial susceptibility that displays results from the previous four weeks as well as annual results. The user could be able to filter results based on dataset parameters.

### Automatic antimicrobial susceptibility profile for patients

Combines the ward's current susceptibility profile and patient data to allow the doctor to easily check what antibiotic medicine they can give to the patient.

  Patient data might include:
  - Relevant risk information related to resistances (for example previous exposure to ESBL, CPE, VRE, OR MRSA)
  - Allergies
  - Kidney and liver function
  - Duration of stay at the ward
  - Medications
  - History of antibiotic medicine
  - Recent travel history
